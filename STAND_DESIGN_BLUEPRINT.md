## 🎨 2. Visual Identity & Color Palette

The stand uses a professional, high-contrast palette communicating medical reliability and technological sophistication:

* **Deep Space Navy (`#0A0E17`):** Base backdrop, conveying serious engineering stability.
* **Electric Cyan (`#38BDF8`):** Primary highlight, matching the neon skeletal computer vision tracker.
* **Biomedical Emerald (`#10B981`):** Secondary accent, signifying active privacy and successful verification.
* **Pure Signal White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-readability typography with minimum 18pt font for all poster copy.

## 🖼️ 3. Poster Specifications & Content Panels

### Panel 1: The Problem (Left Wing)
* **Headline:** *"The Invisible Wall at Public Desks"*
* **Key Statistics:**
  * **70M+** individuals communicate primarily via sign language worldwide.
  * **< 0.1%** of public desk employees possess sign language fluency.
  * **Zero Real-Time Alternatives:** Cloud translators introduce unviable privacy violations and latency.
* **Visual:** Photo/graphic showing a deaf citizen struggling at an emergency hospital triage counter.

### Panel 2: The Core Innovation (Center Banner)
* **Headline:** *"SignBridge Pro: Bidirectional Human-AI Symbiosis"*

  * Microphone $\rightarrow$ Speech-to-Text $\rightarrow$ Natural Language Tokenizer $\rightarrow$ Three.js 3D Avatar.
* **Key Badge:** *"100% Offline • Zero Biometric Storage • GDPR & HIPAA Compliant"*.

### Panel 3: Technical Rigor & Impact (Right Wing)
* **Headline:** *"Engineering Excellence & Social Impact"*
* **Performance Matrix Table:**

## 🃏 4. "Try It Yourself" Interactive Flashcards (Jury Engagement)

Prepare four laminated, high-gloss cards ($15\text{ cm} \times 10\text{ cm}$) placed in an acrylic stand on the front table. Judges can pick up any card and test the system immediately:

### Card 1: "The Greeting"
* **Front:** Clear illustrated diagram of an open palm hand wave.
* **Back:** 
  * *Step 1:* Hold hand facing the camera.
  * *Step 2:* Watch the neon skeleton lock onto your joints.
  * *Expected Output:* *"Hello / Welcome"* vocalized through speakers in $< 20\text{ ms}$.

### Card 2: "The Medical Emergency"
* **Front:** Illustrated gesture of index and middle finger extended (V-shape near chest).
* **Back:** 
  * *Step 1:* Form the peace/medical gesture towards camera.
  * *Expected Output:* *"Where is the medical office?"*

### Card 3: "The Confirmation"
* **Front:** Illustrated thumbs-up gesture.
* **Back:** 
  * *Step 1:* Present clear thumbs-up within the green tracking bounds.
  * *Expected Output:* *"Understood / Approved / Yes"*.

### Card 4: "Reverse Voice Test"
* **Front:** Illustrated microphone icon.
* **Back:** 
  * *Step 1:* Press the blue 'Start Mic' button.
  * *Step 2:* Speak: *"Welcome, please take a seat."*
  * *Expected Output:* Watch the 3D Avatar sign back to you in real-time!

## ✅ 6. Stand Setup & Hardware Checklist

- [ ] **Primary Laptop / Kiosk Terminal:** Running `index.html` at 60 FPS.
- [ ] **External Webcam (Optional but Recommended):** 1080p webcam mounted on an adjustable gooseneck stand at eye level.
- [ ] **External USB Microphone / Speaker:** Clear, loud speaker so judges can clearly hear the synthesized voice even in a noisy exhibition hall.
- [ ] **Secondary Monitor / Tablet (Optional):** Mirrored or extended display facing the presenter to complete the dual-desk aesthetic.
- [ ] **Stand Lighting:** Small USB warm-light ring light directed at the user's hands to guarantee optimal tracking contrast regardless of venue lighting.
- [ ] **Printed Flashcards:** 4 laminated test cards in an acrylic holder.
- [ ] **Deliverables Binder:** Printed and bound documentation package.
- [ ] **Backup Offline USB Drive:** Containing the full offline repository and offline Chromium browser ready to run on any laptop without internet.
